Credit Freezes: What They Block and How to Manage Them

By MoneyMooring Editorial Team · Updated July 27, 2026 · 7 min readEducational content · Official sources checked July 27, 2026

Editorial disclosure: this guide is educational and is not individual financial, legal, tax or insurance advice. MoneyMooring does not sell or recommend a specific financial product.
Key takeaways
  • A security freeze limits prospective creditors’ access to a credit file, which can help prevent new-account identity theft.
  • Federal law gives consumers the right to place and remove freezes for free.
  • A freeze does not stop use of existing accounts or every type of report access.
  • You must contact Equifax, Experian and TransUnion separately.

What a security freeze does, what it does not do and how to place or temporarily lift one with each credit reporting company. The examples below are explanatory, not product quotes or promises of approval, savings, coverage or investment performance.

What a freeze changes

When a freeze is active, a prospective creditor generally cannot access the frozen file to evaluate a new application. Because most lenders will not open an account without that access, a freeze can create a useful barrier against new-account fraud.

You can still review your own reports. Existing creditors and certain government, monitoring, employment, tenant-screening or insurance uses may be treated differently under applicable law.

Freeze, fraud alert and paid lock

ToolCore purposeCost
Security freezeRestricts access for many new-credit decisionsFree by federal law
Fraud alertAsks businesses to verify identity before new creditFree
Credit lockCommercial product that may be bundled with monitoringMay carry a fee

The CFPB notes that paid locks are no more effective than the free security-freeze right.

How to manage the three freezes

  1. Go directly to each nationwide reporting company’s official freeze page.
  2. Use a strong, unique account credential and store recovery information safely.
  3. When applying for credit, ask which report the lender expects to use.
  4. Temporarily lift the relevant freeze for a defined period, then confirm it is active again.

What a freeze does not replace

Continue monitoring existing bank and card accounts, use unique passwords and respond to identity-theft notices. A freeze cannot reverse an unauthorized transaction, stop tax identity theft or prevent takeover of an existing account.

Frequently asked questions

Does freezing a report affect a credit score?

A security freeze restricts access; it does not change the account history used to calculate a score.

Can I apply for an apartment with a freeze?

Tenant screening may use consumer reports outside the federal freeze rule. Ask the screening company or property manager what access is required.

How quickly can an online freeze be lifted?

The CFPB states that a nationwide reporting company generally must remove an online or telephone freeze within one hour of a valid request.
