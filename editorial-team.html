MoneyMooring Editorial Team

The MoneyMooring Editorial Team researches, writes and maintains our plain-English guides to savings, insurance, debt, credit cards and mortgages.

How we work

Each guide starts with primary and authoritative material, including regulator guidance, public product documentation and established market data. We check calculations, distinguish fixed rules from changing rates, and mark time-sensitive figures as indicative.

Review and updates

Every article is reviewed by a human editor for accuracy, clarity and completeness before publication. Articles carry a visible updated date and are reviewed when regulations, product mechanics or market conditions materially change.

Our limits

MoneyMooring is an independent publisher, not a bank, lender, insurer, broker, law firm or registered financial adviser. Our content is educational and cannot account for an individual reader's circumstances.

Corrections and questions

Readers can report an error through our contact page. We investigate corrections promptly and update the article date when a material change is made.
